Short answer

Permanent makeup safety depends on the artist, tools, studio, and client preparation

Permanent makeup involves the skin, so safety matters before, during, and after the appointment. A safer appointment starts with a clean studio, single-use tools, proper barriers, careful pigment handling, and clear aftercare.

At Pomi B. Brow Studio, the Safety page describes a private studio setting, disinfected surfaces, fresh disposable barriers, single-use needles and supplies, pigment containers for single use, and gloves, masks, and protective coverings during appointments.

Quick answer

Permanent makeup is a good fit for many healthy clients when the appointment takes place in a clean, controlled environment with disposable tools and proper aftercare. Some clients should ask questions before booking, especially if they are pregnant, nursing, prone to keloids, taking certain medications, or dealing with skin irritation near the treatment area.

Safety basics

What makes permanent makeup safe?

A safe permanent makeup experience starts with clean practices, proper setup, and a client who is prepared for the appointment and healing period.

Clean studio setup

The appointment space should be cleaned, prepared, and controlled before each client.

Single-use supplies

Needles, cartridges, pigment containers, and disposable tools should not be reused.

Careful technique

The artist should work with focus, precision, and proper handling of tools and pigment.

Aftercare

Following aftercare helps protect the treated area and supports the best healed result.

Single-use tools

Why disposable tools matter

Single-use tools reduce the risk of cross-contamination because the items are used for one client and then safely discarded. This includes needles, cartridges, and other disposable supplies used during the service.

Pomi B. Brow Studio states that needles and cartridges are opened in front of the client, pigment containers are single-use only, and tools and supplies are disposed of after each appointment.

1

New tools

Needles, cartridges, and disposable supplies should be new for the appointment.

2

Opened for the client

Opening tools during the appointment helps show that the items are brand new.

3

Disposed after use

Used disposable tools and supplies should be discarded safely after the session.

Studio practices

Clean studio practices to look for

Permanent makeup should not feel rushed, crowded, or unclear. A clean studio process helps protect the client and allows the artist to focus.

Private appointment setting

A one-client setting helps limit distractions and keeps attention on the person receiving the service. Pomi B. Brow Studio describes the appointment space as private and built for one client at a time.

  • No unnecessary traffic during the service.
  • More control over the appointment environment.
  • More focus during detailed cosmetic work.

Disinfected surfaces and barriers

Surfaces should be cleaned between clients, and disposable barriers should be replaced for each session. These steps help keep the work area organized and sanitary.

  • Surfaces cleaned before and after appointments.
  • Fresh disposable barriers for each session.
  • Protective coverings used during the service.

Gloves, masks, and protective coverings

Gloves, masks, and coverings help keep the appointment cleaner and reduce unnecessary contact with the treatment area and tools.

  • Gloves used during the procedure.
  • Masks and protective coverings used as part of studio practice.
  • Clean handling from start to finish.

Clear aftercare instructions

Safety does not end when the appointment ends. Aftercare helps protect the treated area while it heals and helps support the final result.

  • Know what to avoid while healing.
  • Do not pick, scratch, or peel the area.
  • Ask the studio if you are unsure about healing.
Health considerations

Skin and health considerations before permanent makeup

Most healthy clients are good candidates for permanent makeup, but some people should ask questions before booking or speak with their doctor first.

Pregnant or nursing

Ask before booking if you are pregnant or nursing.

Keloid history

Ask before booking if you are prone to keloid scarring.

Skin irritation

Do not book over active irritation, wounds, or skin concerns in the treatment area.

Medications

Ask before booking if you take blood thinners or medications that affect healing.

When in doubt, ask first

If you have a medical condition, skin condition, recent cosmetic treatment, eye concern, cold sore history, or scalp irritation, ask before booking. For medical questions, speak with your healthcare provider before scheduling.

Who should ask first

Who should ask before booking permanent makeup?

Ask before booking if any of these apply to you. This helps the studio understand your situation and guide you before an appointment.

Brows

Ask before booking microblading, powder brows, or microblading / shading if you have recent brow treatments, active breakouts, irritation, cuts, sunburn, or skin conditions near the brows.

Eyes

Ask before booking eyeliner or lash enhancement if you have current eye irritation, recent eye surgery, certain eye medications, or recent cosmetic eye treatments.

Lips

Ask before booking lip tint if you have active cold sores, frequent cold sore history, lip irritation, recent filler, or healing concerns around the mouth.

Scalp

Ask before booking scalp micropigmentation if you have active scalp irritation, open areas, recent scalp procedures, or a skin condition in the treatment area.

Preparation

How to prepare for a safer permanent makeup appointment

Good preparation helps reduce avoidable issues and supports a smoother appointment. Follow the instructions given for your specific service.

1

Share health details

Tell the studio about medications, medical conditions, skin concerns, allergies, or recent treatments.

2

Avoid common irritants

Follow pre-care instructions about alcohol, caffeine, blood thinners, retinol, exfoliants, tanning, and sunburn.

3

Arrive prepared

Come rested, hydrated, and ready to review your goals, shape, color, and aftercare instructions.

Preparation checklist

  • Review the service you want before booking.
  • Ask about medical or skin concerns before the appointment.
  • Avoid tanning or sunburn before treatment.
  • Follow the pre-care instructions for your service.
  • Plan around the healing period and visible healing signs.

FAQ

Permanent Makeup Safety FAQ

Is permanent makeup safe?

Permanent makeup is safest when performed in a clean studio by a trained professional using disposable tools, careful sanitation, proper setup, and clear aftercare instructions.

What should I look for in a permanent makeup studio?

Look for a clean private setting, single-use tools, fresh disposable barriers, careful pigment handling, gloves, masks, and clear aftercare instructions.

Why are single-use tools important?

Single-use tools help reduce cross-contamination because they are used for one client and then safely discarded.

Who should ask before booking permanent makeup?

Ask before booking if you are pregnant, nursing, prone to keloids, taking blood thinners, have uncontrolled medical conditions, have skin irritation, or recently had treatments near the area.

How should I prepare for a permanent makeup appointment?

Follow the pre-care instructions provided by the studio. In general, share health details, avoid tanning or sunburn, and ask about alcohol, caffeine, blood thinners, retinol, and exfoliants before your appointment.

Is there downtime after permanent makeup?

Most clients should plan for visible healing signs for 1 to 2 weeks. Follow the aftercare instructions for your specific service.
