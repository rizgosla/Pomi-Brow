About the service

What is permanent eyeliner?

Permanent eyeliner is a cosmetic tattoo service that places pigment along the lash line or eyeliner area to create lasting eye definition.

The look can be kept soft and natural or made more defined depending on your eye shape, skin, and preferred style. The goal is to enhance your eyes while keeping the result balanced and wearable.

Eyeliner FAQ

What is permanent eyeliner?

Permanent eyeliner is a cosmetic tattoo service that places pigment along the lash line or eyeliner area to create lasting eye definition.

Can permanent eyeliner look natural?

Yes. Permanent eyeliner can be kept soft and subtle, especially when focused close to the lash line. Your style is discussed before the service begins.

What is the difference between eyeliner and lash enhancement?

Lash enhancement is usually softer and placed close to the lash line for subtle fullness. Eyeliner can create a more visible lined effect, depending on your preferred style.

How long does permanent eyeliner last?

Results vary by skin type, lifestyle, aftercare, and how your skin holds pigment. Most clients need a touch-up to refine the color and shape after the initial appointment.
