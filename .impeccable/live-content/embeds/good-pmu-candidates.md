_Short answer_

## You may be a good candidate if you want natural-looking results and understand the healing process

Permanent makeup is a good fit for many clients who want to simplify their morning routine, improve shape or definition, and feel more put together with less daily makeup.

The best candidates are honest about their skin, health, lifestyle, and expectations. This helps choose the right service and avoid booking at the wrong time.

<!-- pomi-answer-box -->

### How I think about candidacy

I look at three things: your goal, your skin, and your lifestyle. A service should fit how you naturally like to look. Brows, eyeliner, lash enhancement, lip tint, and scalp micropigmentation all have different healing needs, so the right choice should feel practical for you.

<!-- pomi-wrap -->

_Brows_

## Good candidates for brow services

Brow services are a good fit if you want better shape, more fullness, or less time filling in your brows.

<!-- pomi-card -->

### Sparse brows

If your brows look thin or patchy, permanent brow services may help create a fuller look.

### Uneven brows

If your brows do not match well, brow mapping helps create better balance before the service begins.

### Daily brow makeup

If you fill in your brows every morning, permanent brows may save time.

### Soft or defined style

Your brow service should match whether you want hair-like detail, soft shading, or a fuller makeup look.

<!-- pomi-wrap -->

_Eyes_

## Good candidates for eyeliner and lash enhancement

Eye services are a good fit if you want your eyes to look more defined with less daily makeup.

<!-- pomi-wide-card -->

### Lash enhancement may fit you if...

You want the base of your lashes to look darker and fuller, but you do not want a visible eyeliner look. This is the softer, more natural eye option.

- You like a no-makeup look.
- You want subtle eye definition.
- You want fuller-looking lashes.
- You rarely wear eyeliner.

### Permanent eyeliner may fit you if...

You already wear eyeliner often and want a more defined eye shape without applying liner every day.

- You like a more polished eye look.
- Your eyeliner smudges or transfers.
- You want more definition than lash enhancement.
- You want your liner to look more even.

<!-- pomi-wrap -->

_Lips_

## Good candidates for lip tint

Lip tint is best for clients who want soft color, better definition, and a fresher natural lip tone.

<!-- pomi-card -->

### Pale lips

Lip tint may help add soft color so your lips look more awake.

### Uneven tone

If your lips have uneven color, a custom tint may help the tone look more balanced.

### Soft definition

Lip tint helps the lip shape look more defined without a harsh liner look.

### Less lipstick

If you want a little color without applying lipstick all the time, lip tint may fit your routine.

<!-- pomi-answer-box -->

### Important for lips

If you have ever had a cold sore, please ask before booking lip tint. Lip tattooing may trigger a cold sore for some clients, so it is better to talk about it ahead of time.

<!-- pomi-wrap -->

_Hair_

## Good candidates for scalp micropigmentation

Scalp micropigmentation is a good fit for clients who want the look of more hair density or a more defined hairline.

<!-- pomi-card -->

### Thinning areas

SMP may help reduce the look of visible scalp through thinning hair.

### Hairline definition

If your hairline looks less defined, SMP may help create a cleaner-looking shape.

### Scalp contrast

Small pigment impressions help reduce contrast between hair and scalp.

### Low-maintenance density

SMP may reduce the need for temporary powders or hair fibers.

<!-- pomi-wrap -->

_Ask first_

## Who should ask before booking?

Some clients are still good candidates, but timing matters. Please ask first if any of these apply to you.

<!-- pomi-wide-card -->

### Health and healing

- You are pregnant or nursing.
- You take blood thinners.
- You are prone to keloids.
- You have a medical condition that affects healing.
- You have allergies or past reactions to cosmetic products.

### Skin or treatment area concerns

- You have irritation, sunburn, cuts, breakouts, or open skin near the area.
- You recently had filler, Botox, chemical peels, laser, or other cosmetic treatments.
- You have active cold sores or a cold sore history for lip tint.
- You have eye irritation or recent eye treatments for eyeliner or lash enhancement.
- You have scalp irritation for scalp micropigmentation.

<!-- pomi-answer-box -->

### My advice

If you are unsure, ask before booking. It is better to check first than to arrive and find out we need to wait. For medical questions, speak with your healthcare provider before scheduling.

_Skin type_

## Skin type considerations

Your skin affects how permanent makeup heals and how long the color holds.

<!-- pomi-card -->

### Oily skin

Oily skin may heal softer or fade faster with certain brow techniques. Powder or shading may be a better fit for some clients.

### Dry skin

Dry skin may hold fine detail well, but aftercare still matters during healing.

### Mature skin

Mature skin needs a gentle, realistic approach. Softer designs often heal better than harsh shapes.

### Sensitive skin

Sensitive skin may need extra care, slower timing, or a conversation before booking.

<!-- pomi-wrap -->

_Lifestyle_

## Lifestyle considerations before booking

Permanent makeup works best when the service fits your daily life and you are able to follow aftercare.

<!-- pomi-step -->

1

### Your daily makeup style

If you like soft makeup, choose a softer service. If you like more definition, choose a service that matches that look.

2

### Your healing schedule

Plan around events, photos, travel, workouts, pools, saunas, and sun exposure while healing.

3

### Your maintenance plan

Permanent makeup softens over time. Touch-ups help maintain color, shape, and definition.

_Choosing a service_

## How to choose the right permanent makeup service

Start with the result you want, then choose the service that matches your features, skin, and routine.

<!-- pomi-wide-card -->

### If you want better brows

Ask about the difference between natural hair-like strokes, soft powder brows, and a fuller combination brow look.

### If you want eye definition

Ask whether lash enhancement or permanent eyeliner fits your eye shape, makeup style, and comfort level.

### If you want lip color

Ask about lip tint if your lips look pale, uneven, or undefined and you want a soft natural color.

### If you want fuller-looking hair density

Ask about scalp micropigmentation if you want the look of more coverage in thinning areas or a cleaner hairline shape.

_FAQ_

## Permanent Makeup Candidate FAQ

<!-- pomi-faq -->

#### Who is a good candidate for permanent makeup?

A good candidate wants natural-looking enhancement, understands the healing process, follows aftercare, and does not have active skin or health concerns that would make timing unsafe.

#### Who should ask before booking?

Ask before booking if you are pregnant, nursing, taking blood thinners, prone to keloids, have active irritation, have a cold sore history for lip tint, or recently had cosmetic treatments near the area.

#### Am I a good candidate for brow services?

You may be a good candidate if you want better brow shape, more fullness, or less daily brow makeup. The best brow technique depends on your skin and preferred style.

#### Am I a good candidate for lip tint?

You may be a good candidate if you want soft lip color, better tone, and natural definition. Ask first if you have a cold sore history or active irritation.

#### Am I a good candidate for permanent eyeliner or lash enhancement?

You may be a good candidate if you want more eye definition and do not have active eye irritation or recent eye procedures. Ask first if you use eye medications or have recent eye treatments.

#### Does skin type matter?

Yes. Oily, dry, mature, sensitive, or textured skin may heal differently. Your skin type helps guide the best service and technique.
