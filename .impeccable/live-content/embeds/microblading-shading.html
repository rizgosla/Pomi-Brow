About the service

What is Microblading / Shading?

Microblading / Shading is a combination brow service. Microblading creates fine hair-like strokes, while shading adds a soft powder effect to create more density and definition.

The goal is to keep the brows soft and natural while adding enough fullness to improve shape, balance, and everyday polish.

FAQ

Microblading / Shading FAQ

What is the difference between microblading and Microblading / Shading?

Microblading uses fine hair-like strokes. Microblading / Shading combines those strokes with soft shading to create more fullness, density, and definition.

Who is Microblading / Shading best for?

This service is a strong fit if you want a natural brow look but need more fullness than hair strokes alone provide. It works well for sparse, uneven, or patchy brows.

Does Microblading / Shading look natural?

Yes. The goal is a soft, balanced brow. The hair strokes create natural detail, while the shading adds fullness without making the brows look too heavy.

How long does Microblading / Shading last?

Results vary by skin type, lifestyle, aftercare, and how your skin holds pigment. Most clients need a touch-up to refine the color, shape, and softness after the initial appointment.
