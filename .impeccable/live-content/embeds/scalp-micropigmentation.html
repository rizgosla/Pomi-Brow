About the service

What is scalp micropigmentation?

Scalp micropigmentation, also called SMP, is a cosmetic tattoo service that uses tiny pigment impressions to create the look of hair follicles on the scalp.

The goal is to make thinning areas look denser, help the hairline look more defined, and create a natural-looking result that blends with your existing hair and skin tone.
