About the service

What are powder brows?

Powder brows, also called ombre powder brows, use a soft shading technique to create the look of fuller brows. The front of the brow usually stays softer, while the arch and tail look more defined.

The healed result often looks similar to a soft brow powder or pencil finish. The goal is to enhance your natural features, improve shape, and create a brow look that fits your face.

Healing expectations

What should you expect after powder brows?

Powder brows usually look darker and more defined at first. As the skin heals, the color softens and the brows begin to look more natural.

During healing, brows may look dry, flaky, lighter, or uneven before the final color settles. The touch-up appointment helps refine the color, shape, and softness.

FAQ

Are Powder Brows For Me? FAQ

Are powder brows good for sparse brows?

Yes. Powder brows are often a strong option for sparse or patchy brows because the shading helps create the look of more fullness and structure.

Do powder brows look natural?

Powder brows can look soft and natural when the shape, color, and density are customized. The result is usually more filled-in than microblading, but it does not need to look heavy.

Are powder brows better than microblading?

One is not better for everyone. Powder brows are better if you want a shaded makeup-like finish. Microblading is better if you want fine hair-like strokes. Microblading / shading combines both effects.

Who should choose powder brows?

Powder brows may fit you if you want more brow definition, a soft filled-in look, better symmetry, and less daily brow makeup.

Will powder brows look too dark at first?

Fresh powder brows often look darker and more defined at first. The color usually softens as the brows heal.

Do powder brows need a touch-up?

Most clients need a touch-up to refine the healed color, shape, and softness. The touch-up helps complete the final result.
