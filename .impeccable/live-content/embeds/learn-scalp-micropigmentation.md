_Short answer_

## Scalp micropigmentation helps create the look of fuller hair without surgery

Scalp micropigmentation, often called SMP, uses tiny pigment dots on the scalp to make thinning areas look denser. It does not grow new hair. It creates the appearance of more coverage by reducing the contrast between the scalp and existing hair.

This service may help clients with thinning hair, visible scalp, soft hairline loss, sparse areas, or areas where they want a more filled-in look.

<!-- pomi-answer-box -->

### How I explain SMP to clients

Think of SMP like soft shading for the scalp. The goal is not to make the scalp look painted. The goal is to place tiny impressions in a way that blends with your hair color, scalp tone, and natural pattern.

_Who it helps_

## Who is scalp micropigmentation for?

SMP is for clients who want the appearance of more density, better scalp coverage, or a cleaner-looking hairline.

<!-- pomi-wide-card -->

### Clients with thinning hair

If your scalp shows through your hair, SMP may help create the look of more density. The tiny pigment impressions reduce the light contrast between the scalp and hair.

### Clients with a softer or receding hairline

If your hairline looks less defined than it used to, SMP may help create a cleaner-looking shape. The goal is to keep the hairline believable and natural for your face.

### Clients who use powders or hair fibers

If you use temporary products to hide scalp visibility, SMP may offer a lower-maintenance cosmetic option.

### Clients who want a non-surgical option

SMP does not involve moving hair or growing new hair. It is a cosmetic density service that changes how the scalp looks.

_SMP vs surgery_

## How is SMP different from hair transplant surgery?

Scalp micropigmentation and hair transplant surgery are completely different. SMP is a cosmetic tattoo service. It creates the look of hair density by adding pigment to the scalp. It does not move hair, grow hair, or change the number of hair follicles.

Hair transplant surgery is a medical procedure that moves hair follicles from one area to another. If you are considering surgery, speak with a medical provider. If you want a cosmetic density effect without surgery, SMP may be worth discussing.

_Who should ask first_

## Who should ask before booking SMP?

Please ask before booking if there is anything going on with your scalp or health that may affect the appointment.

<!-- pomi-wide-card -->

### Scalp concerns

- You have active scalp irritation.
- You have open skin, sores, or sunburn on the scalp.
- You have active flaking or a skin condition in the treatment area.
- You recently had scalp treatments, injections, or hair transplant work.

### Health and healing concerns

- You are pregnant or nursing.
- You take blood thinners.
- You are prone to keloids.
- You have a medical condition that affects healing.

<!-- pomi-answer-box -->

### My advice

If you are not sure whether your scalp is ready, ask first. It is always better to wait and do the service at the right time than to work over irritated skin.
