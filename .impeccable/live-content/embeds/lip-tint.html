About the service

What is lip tint?

Lip tint is a permanent makeup service that places pigment into the lips to create soft color, improved shape, and a more even-looking tone.

The goal is not to create a heavy lipstick look. The goal is to enhance your natural lips with a shade that looks fresh, soft, and wearable every day.

FAQ

Lip Tint FAQ

What is lip tint?

Lip tint is a permanent makeup service that adds soft pigment to the lips for natural-looking color, better definition, and a more even tone.

Does lip tint look natural?

Yes. Lip tint can be customized for a soft, natural-looking finish. The goal is to enhance your lips, not create a heavy lipstick look.

Who is lip tint best for?

Lip tint is a strong fit if your lips look pale, uneven, undefined, or if you want soft everyday color without applying lipstick or liner every day.

How long does lip tint last?

Results vary by skin type, lifestyle, aftercare, natural lip color, and how your skin holds pigment. Most clients need a touch-up to refine color and softness after the initial appointment.
