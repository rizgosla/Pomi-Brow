About the service

What is lash enhancement?

Lash enhancement is a permanent makeup service that places pigment close to the lash line to create the look of fuller, darker lashes.

Unlike traditional eyeliner, lash enhancement is designed to look softer and more natural. The goal is to define the eyes while keeping the result subtle and wearable.

Lash Enhancement FAQ

What is lash enhancement?

Lash enhancement is a permanent makeup service that places pigment close to the lash line to create a fuller-looking lash base and soft eye definition.

What is the difference between lash enhancement and eyeliner?

Lash enhancement is softer and placed close to the lash line for subtle fullness. Eyeliner creates a more visible lined effect, depending on your preferred style.

Does lash enhancement look natural?

Yes. Lash enhancement is designed for a subtle, natural-looking result. It helps your lashes look darker and fuller without a heavy makeup look.

How long does lash enhancement last?

Results vary by skin type, lifestyle, aftercare, and how your skin holds pigment. Most clients need a touch-up to refine the color and softness after the initial appointment.
