_About the service_

## What is scalp micropigmentation?

Scalp micropigmentation, also called SMP, is a cosmetic tattoo service that uses tiny pigment impressions to create the look of hair follicles on the scalp.

The goal is to make thinning areas look denser, help the hairline look more defined, and create a natural-looking result that blends with your existing hair and skin tone.

## Scalp Micropigmentation FAQ

<!-- pomi-faq -->

#### What is scalp micropigmentation?

Scalp micropigmentation is a cosmetic tattoo service that places tiny pigment impressions on the scalp to create the look of fuller hair density.

#### Who is scalp micropigmentation best for?

SMP is a strong fit if you want to improve the appearance of thinning areas, scalp visibility, hairline definition, or overall hair density.

#### Does scalp micropigmentation look natural?

Yes. The goal is to use small pigment impressions that blend with your existing hair, skin tone, and desired density level.

#### How many SMP sessions do I need?

The number of sessions depends on the size of the treatment area, the amount of density needed, and the final result you want. A consultation helps estimate the best plan.

#### How much does scalp micropigmentation cost?

#### Who should ask before booking scalp micropigmentation?
