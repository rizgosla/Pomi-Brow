Ombre Powder Brows are a semi-permanent brow service designed to create a soft shaded effect. The front of the brow stays lighter, while the middle and tail look more defined.

The healed result is similar to a soft brow powder or pencil look. The shape is customized before the service begins, so the final brows fit your face and your preferred style.

## Ombre Powder Brows FAQ

<!-- pomi-faq -->

#### What is the difference between microblading and Ombre Powder Brows?

Microblading creates fine hair-like strokes. Ombre Powder Brows create a soft shaded effect, similar to brow powder or pencil. Ombre Powder Brows are a strong fit if you prefer a more filled-in brow look.

#### Do Ombre Powder Brows look natural?

Yes. The result is customized to your face, brow shape, and preferred finish. The front of the brow is kept softer while the arch and tail look more defined.

#### How long do Ombre Powder Brows last?

Results vary by skin type, lifestyle, aftercare, and how your skin holds pigment. Most clients need a touch-up to refine color, shape, and softness after the first appointment.

#### Is the touch-up included?

#### Where is Pomi B. Brow Studio located?

#### Who should ask before booking Ombre Powder Brows?

Ask before booking if you are pregnant, nursing, taking blood thinners, prone to keloids, undergoing certain skin treatments, or have a skin condition near the brow area.
