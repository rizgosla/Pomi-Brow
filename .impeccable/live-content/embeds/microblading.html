About the service

What is microblading?

Microblading is a semi-permanent brow service where fine, hair-like strokes are placed into the brow area to create a fuller and more balanced look.

The goal is not to change your face. The goal is to frame your features with brows suited to you. At Pomi B. Brow Studio, the result is soft, symmetrical, and natural-looking.

FAQ

Microblading FAQ

How long does microblading last?

Results vary by skin type, lifestyle, aftercare, and how your skin holds pigment. Most clients need a touch-up to refine the color and shape after the initial appointment.

Does microblading look natural?

Yes. The goal at Pomi B. Brow Studio is a soft, natural-looking brow. The shape is mapped to your face, and the strokes are designed to blend with your natural brows.

What is the difference between microblading and Ombre Powder Brows?

Microblading creates fine hair-like strokes for a natural brow-hair look. Ombre Powder Brows create a soft shaded effect, similar to brow powder or pencil.

Who should ask before booking microblading?

Ask before booking if you are pregnant, nursing, taking blood thinners, prone to keloids, undergoing certain skin treatments, or have a skin condition near the brow area.
