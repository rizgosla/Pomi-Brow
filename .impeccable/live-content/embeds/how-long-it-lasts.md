_Short answer_

## Permanent makeup can last for months to years, but it is not truly permanent forever

Permanent makeup gradually fades because pigment softens as your skin heals, renews, and reacts to lifestyle factors. Brows, eyeliner, lash enhancement, lip tint, and scalp micropigmentation can all fade at different speeds.

A touch-up helps refresh color, refine shape, and keep the result looking balanced. Your exact timeline depends on your skin type, aftercare, sun exposure, skincare products, and how your skin holds pigment.

<!-- pomi-answer-box -->

### Quick answer by service

Brow services usually need regular maintenance because facial skin renews and brows are exposed to skincare and sun. Eyeliner and lash enhancement can soften over time but often fade more gradually. Lip tint can fade differently depending on natural lip color, lifestyle, and healing. Scalp micropigmentation maintenance depends on the treatment area, density, and sun exposure.

Microblading usually lasts between 1 to 3 years, but the microblading duration can vary based on factors like your skin type, lifestyle, skincare routine, sun exposure, and especially your microblading aftercare. Some clients may notice they need a color refresh after 12 to 18 months, while others can maintain good color for a longer period, particularly if they have normal to dry skin, protect their brows from sun exposure, and diligently follow aftercare instructions. Since microblading is considered semi-permanent, the pigment will fade over time, meaning your brows won’t retain the same color and shape indefinitely. Scheduling a touch-up or annual refresh is essential to help maintain the desired results.

#### Why Microblading Fades Over Time

Microblading places pigment into the upper layers of the skin, and understanding how long does microblading last is important for your expectations. As your skin naturally renews itself, the pigment slowly softens and fades, which is a normal process and does not indicate that something went wrong. Factors influencing microblading duration include your skin type, natural brow hair, immune system, skincare products, sun exposure, sweating and lifestyle, aftercare, the color used, and how your skin holds pigment. Some clients retain pigment longer, while others may experience faster fading. During your appointment, Pomi will explain what to expect based on your unique skin and brow goals, including insights on microblading aftercare to help maintain your results.

#### Skin Type Makes a Big Difference

Your skin type significantly influences how long does microblading last.

Normal to dry skin:

Microblading often heals with cleaner hair strokes and longer-lasting definition on normal to dry skin. This skin type usually holds microblading well, contributing to an extended microblading duration.

Oily skin:

For those with oily skin, the microblading aftercare becomes crucial, as oily skin often fades microblading faster. Hair strokes may heal softer, blur sooner, or lose crisp detail over time. If you have oily skin, Pomi might recommend ombre powder brows or combo brows as alternatives.

Combination skin:

Combination skin presents variability; some areas hold pigment well, while the oilier zones may fade faster, impacting how long does microblading last.

Mature skin:

Mature skin requires a gentle approach. Soft color, careful shaping, and realistic expectations are essential for effective microblading aftercare. Pomi will recommend the best brow technique based on your skin texture and desired look.

#### How Long Before You Need a Touch-Up?

Microblading is typically a two-step process that leads to beautiful, lasting brows. The first appointment is focused on creating the shape, color, and foundation for your brows. After this initial session, many clients wonder how long does microblading last and how to ensure the best results. That’s where the perfecting touch-up comes in. This follow-up appointment helps refine the healed result after your skin has recovered. It's crucial for microblading aftercare, as some areas might heal lighter than others. The perfecting touch-up helps improve balance, color, and shape, ensuring your brows look their best for the duration of the microblading effects.

#### When Should You Book an Annual Brow Refresh?

Many clients book an annual brow refresh around 12 to 18 months after their original microblading service, as they may wonder how long does microblading last. You might be ready for a refresh if: Your brows look lighter than you want, the shape looks less defined, you fill in your brows more often, the tails have faded, one brow faded more than the other, the color looks too soft, or you want your brows cleaned up before a trip, event, or new season. For existing clients, an annual brow refresh not only helps keep the brows soft, balanced, and natural, but it also aligns with proper microblading aftercare practices, ensuring the longevity of your results throughout the microblading duration.

#### What Makes Microblading Fade Faster?

Microblading duration can be affected by how quickly the skin renews or how soon the pigment breaks down. Understanding how long does microblading last is essential for maintaining your brows. Common reasons that lead to faster fading include: oily skin, frequent sun exposure, tanning, swimming, heavy sweating, chemical peels, retinol or tretinoin use near the brow area, exfoliating acids close to the brows, laser treatments, not adhering to microblading aftercare, and picking or scratching during the healing process. Additionally, certain medications or health conditions can impact the longevity of your microblading results. While you don’t need to abandon your regular skincare routine forever, it’s advisable to avoid strong exfoliating products directly on the brow area to help ensure your results last longer.

#### How to Help Your Microblading Last Longer

Good care helps protect your results and can significantly influence how long microblading lasts. To help your brows last longer and ensure optimal microblading aftercare, consider the following tips: Follow Pomi’s aftercare instructions, avoid picking or scratching while healing, and keep your brows protected from heavy sun exposure. Additionally, it’s best to avoid tanning beds, refrain from applying retinol, tretinoin, or exfoliating acids directly over healed brows, and use sunscreen around the brow area after healing. Be cautious with harsh facial treatments directly over the brows and remember to book refresh appointments before the pigment fades too much. Small habits make a difference in the microblading duration; sun exposure and exfoliating skincare are two of the biggest reasons brows fade sooner.

#### What Happens If You Wait Too Long?

If you wait too long, you may wonder how long does microblading last, as the brows might fade enough that a simple refresh is no longer the best option. When pigment fades excessively, Pomi might need to rebuild more of the brow shape. If old pigment has changed color, turning gray, red, or uneven, correction work may be necessary. This is why annual refresh appointments are crucial for proper microblading aftercare. They help maintain the brows before the shape and color fade too much, ensuring you get the best results throughout the microblading duration.

#### Microblading vs Powder Brows: Which Lasts Longer?

Powder brows often last longer than microblading, especially for those with oily skin. When considering how long does microblading last, it's important to note that microblading creates fine hair-like strokes that provide a natural appearance. However, the longevity of these strokes may diminish more quickly on oily or textured skin, making microblading aftercare crucial. On the other hand, powder brows create a soft shaded effect and tend to hold better for clients with oily skin, sparse brows, or those seeking more definition. Combo brows, which utilize both hair strokes and shading, are ideal for clients desiring a natural look with added fullness and enhanced color retention. A simple guide: Choose microblading if you want the most natural hair-stroke look. Opt for powder brows if you prefer a softly filled-in look or have oily skin. Select combo brows if you want hair strokes combined with fuller definition. Pomi will recommend the right brow service based on your skin type, natural brow hair, and desired result.
